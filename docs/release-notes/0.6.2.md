This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **No more green dot at higher render scales.** The tiny triangle at the screen centre is gone. #7
- **Mouse & keyboard preset keeps its bindings.** You can now set it up in one click from Controls. #5
- **Quick setup: Use twin-stick controller.** #5
- **Main menu reads menu rows under separate menu buttons.** #2
- **Button prompts.**
  - Icons are drawn at twice the game's size and centred on the text line.
  - On the Trilogy disc: Spider Ball shows R, Morph Ball C reads as X, and the rest of the Wii button art is mapped.
- **Map rotation invert options.**
- **Simpler Controls page.** Removed the Swap Scan/Echo visor buttons option, the Weapon buttons toggle and the empty Mouse buttons section.
- **Behaviour fixes** to match the original game: Sandworm, SandBoss, Ing enemies, WallCrawler, Dark Guardian knee armour, Boost Guardian, the player turret, and more.
- **Flatpak** now uses the supported `org.freedesktop.Platform` 25.08 runtime (24.08 is end-of-life).
