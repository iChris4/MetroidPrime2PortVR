This is an early release. Please report what you hit and attach the log (F1 > System > Log).

**You need your own disc image:** Metroid Prime 2: Echoes USA (`G2ME01`), PAL (`G2MP01`), or Trilogy (`R3ME01` USA, `R3MP01` PAL). No disc image or game assets are included.

## What changed

- **Rapid fire sounds in sync.** Sounds now play within 10 ms instead of about 80 ms late. #10
- **Scan text reads the Menu A button**, and the lock-on keeps its own binding. #11
- **Twin stick: R no longer free-looks outside the Morph Ball.** #12
- **Remap page shows the MP2 beam and visor names.** #12
- **Dark and Light Beam ammo drops from caches.** #13
- **Volume sliders reach 100%.** #14
- **Twin stick beam switching.** The right stick no longer changes beams; hold LB and press D-pad left for the Light Beam or right for the Dark Beam, as on the C-stick. Also works with the touch overlay on. #15
- **Save states** no longer turn back on layers the state had switched off.
